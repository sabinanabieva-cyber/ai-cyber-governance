
# AI Cyber Governance

This repository presents my work at the intersection of artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and cyber policy. It includes case studies, analytical reports, and original scenarios examining how AI-driven technologies introduce new security risks and governance challenges.

The work reflects a progression from academic analysis to independent research and applied scenario design, with a focus on real-world implications and policy-oriented thinking.

---

## Scope

This repository focuses on:

* AI-enabled cyber threats, including phishing, deepfakes, and automated systems
* Ethical decision-making in cybersecurity contexts
* Legal and regulatory considerations in AI deployment
* Human behavior and risk in security systems
* Policy design for emerging technologies

---

## Structure

assigned-case-studies/
Analyses based on scenarios provided in an academic setting. All reasoning, evaluation, and conclusions are original.

original-case-studies/
Independently developed scenarios exploring emerging risks and unresolved governance questions.

policy-memos/
Targeted policy recommendations addressing issues identified in case analyses.

simulations/
Scenario-based exercises designed for group discussion and cybersecurity education programs.

frameworks/
Ethical, legal, and analytical frameworks used across the repository.

---

## Method

Each case study or report follows a consistent analytical approach:

1. Context and scenario definition
2. Identification of stakeholders
3. Ethical problem framing
4. Application of relevant frameworks
5. Legal and policy analysis
6. Recommendations and implications

---

## Purpose

This repository serves as a foundation for ongoing work in AI governance and cybersecurity. It also supports the development of structured, scenario-based learning modules for future cybersecurity education initiatives.
